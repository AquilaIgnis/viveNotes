A handwritten note-taking app built for students, combining the natural feel of pen and paper with the power of digital documents.

Every feature is free, cross-device sync included. Your notes stay private: no data leaves your device, and all AI runs on it.

Nothing leaves the device

Notes, OCR, handwriting recognition and the math engine all run locally. There is no telemetry and no account to make.

Sync you host yourself

Cross-device sync is free and always was. Point it at your own sync server and the data never touches anyone else's.

Built for a stylus

Pressure, smoothing, configurable pens, hardware keyboard shortcuts and stylus button mappings — tuned on real tablets.

Features

Everything, and none of it upsold.

Fuzzy notebook search

One query across typed text, tables, handwriting, and text found inside images.

Handwriting & formula recognition

On-device recognition with math solving, evaluation and graphing built on top of it.

Ink that keeps up

7 ms frames on pages carrying more than ten thousand strokes.

Infinite free-form canvas

Pages grow in any direction — no fixed sheet to run out of.

Rich-text editing

Headings, lists, checkboxes, styles, tables and images alongside your ink.

Native LaTeX

Inline and free-form equations, rendered natively rather than as images.

On-device image OCR

Drop in a photo or a slide; its text becomes searchable like everything else.

Custom paper

Ruled, dotted, hexagonal, and grids at several sizes.

Snapshots & revisions

The database snapshots itself automatically, and any revision can be restored.

Open export format

Everything exports to .vive, a documented format you can read yourself.

Keyboard & stylus mapping

Hardware shortcuts, and stylus buttons bound to the tools you actually use.

Free, all of it

No subscription, no paid tier, no feature held back for one.

Math engine

Write it by hand.
Get an answer back.

Lasso a handwritten expression and Vive Notes reads it into LaTeX, then hands it to a full symbolic engine. Matrices, series, integrals and equations all get the same treatment.

  • Transpose, RREF, rank, inverse, eigenvalues and null space
  • Evaluate, solve, and test series convergence
  • Copy out the LaTeX, or copy the result
  • Powered on-device by SymPy through Chaquopy

Documents

A word processor sharing the page with your pen.

Typed notes, headings, bullet lists, highlights and images live on the same canvas as your handwriting — so a lecture slide, its summary, and your margin scribbles stay together.

  • Full text styling, alignment, lists and checkboxes
  • Images with searchable OCR'd text
  • Inline equations wherever the cursor is
